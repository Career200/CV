I do my best work in those in-between spaces — I'm happiest when I reduce friction for people. When the shared library shifts under the apps that lean on it, I'm the one **absorbing the breakage** so nobody downstream has to — migrations, tests, deprecation paths that let everyone move naturally while the floor changes underneath them. And I keep the *boring promises*: things save when they should, access stays locked where it must, and every codebase comes out a little more navigable than I found it.
