Turns out it's fun! I've led an engineering team at a technical academy, guiding new developers through building production-grade full-stack applications with TypeScript, React, Node.js, and PostgreSQL. Two-month agile cycle teaching architecture, best practices, team dynamics, the whole development process. Best outcome? **Mentees landing professional engineering roles.** And a sidestep - teaching young kids the basics of programming through game development with Scratch; explaining code to 7-year-olds is *intense*, but the absolute delight when they see their work come to life is priceless.
