My name is Dmitriy Zhukov, and I can't stop thinking about _interfaces_. Between the app and the user, between the data and the app, between a component and its use-site, between a developer and the codebase, and so on.

Most of my work has been in dense scientific software, where I built the **in-house component library** that ties a suite of specialist apps together, and _dragged_ every one of them closer to the modern web. The rest of me goes into tabletop RPGs, fitness and music.
